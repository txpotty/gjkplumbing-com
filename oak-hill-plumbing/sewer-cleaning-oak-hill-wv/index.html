    Older properties may still have vitrified clay or even Orangeburg fiber pipe running to the street. Clay joints are open invitations for roots, and Orangeburg can flatten under soil weight.     Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Oak Hill, WV

    Many older homes have cast iron drains under the slab or floor. West Virginia has a lot of older housing, and many homes built before 1960 still have galvanized supply lines and cast iron drains. Over time the inside of cast iron corrodes, scales and develops a channel along the bottom. Aggressive cutters can break thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting suited to older iron. This restores flow and gives a clear view on camera of the remaining wall. It also helps you decide if lining is a smart option.
