    Softener Loop Installation in Oak Hill

    A softener loop is the plumbing connection that routes your main water line through a softener. Many newer homes include a loop, but older homes often do not. We cut into the main line, install the loop and add a bypass valve. We keep outdoor hose bibs and irrigation on hard water when possible to save salt. A proper loop makes future service simple.

    For homeowners looking for an alternative to traditional softeners, we also offer salt-free water conditioner installation.     Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Oak Hill

These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. GJK Plumbing carefully installs many models of salt-based softeners, making sure of proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Oak Hill home.
