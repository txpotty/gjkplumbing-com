    If your home has old, corroded or leaking pipes, a full home PEX repipe can greatly improve your plumbing system. This prevents future leaks, improves water pressure and ends the rust-colored water often seen with older piping in Oak Hill.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Oak Hill

    GJK Plumbing frequently installs Uponor PEX systems, a leading brand known for high-quality, durable PEX-A piping. Uponor's ProPEX expansion fittings create strong, reliable and leak-resistant connections, ideal for the demands of a home's plumbing.     PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Oak Hill

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a solid option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer dependable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to verify a proper seal.

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Oak Hill

    When your main water line from the street to your home needs replacement, PEX is an excellent option. Its flexibility allows for easier trenchless or minimal-dig installations, reducing disruption to your yard in Oak Hill. PEX main lines handle ground movement and resist corrosion better than rigid pipe. Rocky, steep terrain is common across West Virginia, and many homes sit on hillsides where drainage and slope movement affect buried lines.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Oak Hill, WV

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is notorious for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Many insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to sell and insure.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Oak Hill, WV

    GJK Plumbing is experienced with installing all types of PEX piping, including PEX-A, PEX-B and PEX-C, each with distinct properties for specific applications. We help you choose the best PEX type for your home's needs and budget in Oak Hill, WV. Our expertise ensures the correct PEX material and fitting method, whether it is expansion for PEX-A or crimp and clamp for PEX-B and PEX-C, for a secure and lasting system.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Oak Hill, WV

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Oak Hill, WV

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, much like a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when several fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to weigh cost and performance.
