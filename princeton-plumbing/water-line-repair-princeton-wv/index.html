    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Princeton

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We verify the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to locate the exact spot.     Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Princeton

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a particularly challenging issue. Aging galvanized pipe narrows with rust and scale over time, causing low water pressure, leaks and discolored water. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to serious structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to locate slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak quickly in Princeton is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.
