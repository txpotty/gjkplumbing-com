    Tank Water Heater Flushing in Princeton

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clean. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs quieter.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Maintenance in Princeton

    A standard tank water heater flush and inspection in Princeton usually costs $100 to $250. Tankless descaling typically runs $150 to $300, and anode rod replacement adds about $75 to $200 including the part.

    Most manufacturers and plumbing experts recommend annual maintenance for your water heater. In homes with harder water, flushing every 6 to 12 months can be helpful to prevent sediment buildup.

    The anode rod is a metal rod inside your water heater that sacrifices itself to keep the steel tank from corroding. It attracts corrosive elements in the water. Regular inspection and replacement of a depleted anode rod are key for extending the lifespan of your water heater, particularly where water quality varies.

    During a water heater flush, we drain the tank completely and then refill it, letting water flow out through the drain valve until it runs clear. This process removes mineral deposits and sediment that collect at the bottom of the tank, which can hurt efficiency and cause damage.
