    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Princeton, WV

    Calcium scale is the leading cause of tankless heater failure. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year.
