    Most fixture drains are cleared in 30 to 60 minutes. Main line jobs usually take 1 to 2 hours, longer if roots are heavy or there is no cleanout.

    Most standard drain cleaning for a single fixture can be done within 1 to 2 hours. More extensive jobs, such as main sewer line clearing or hydro jetting, may take 3 to 4 hours or longer, depending on the difficulty of the clog.

    We usually advise against chemical drain cleaners. While they might offer a temporary fix, they can corrode pipes, especially older ones, and are often ineffective against tough clogs like tree roots or heavy grease buildup. They can also pose health risks.

    Caustic and acid cleaners can damage older cast iron, soften plastic joints and harm the person using them. They also rarely clear the full clog. Mechanical cleaning is the safer choice and more effective.

    For most homes, an annual or twice-yearly professional drain cleaning is enough, especially for kitchen and main lines. If you have frequent clogs or an older plumbing system, more frequent cleanings might be helpful to prevent major issues.

    Water hardness in West Virginia ranges from moderate to hard in many areas, and mineral scale is a common issue for water heaters and fixtures.
