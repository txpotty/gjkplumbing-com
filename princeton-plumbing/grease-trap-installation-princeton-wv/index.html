    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Princeton

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with tight space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid easily.

    Grease Trap Installation Cost in Princeton, WV

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Princeton

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, usually under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate. GJK Plumbing installs various sizes of HGI units, ensuring proper venting, leveling, and connection to your drainage system in Princeton for maximum efficiency.
