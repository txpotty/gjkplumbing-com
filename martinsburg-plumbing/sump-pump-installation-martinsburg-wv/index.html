    New Sump Pit Installation in Martinsburg

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can create one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, hardpan or rock and set a durable basin.     Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Martinsburg

    The cost of sump pump installation in Martinsburg, WV usually ranges from $600 to $1,800, depending on the pump type, whether a new pit needs to be dug and the complexity of the plumbing and electrical work.     Most sump pumps last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that sit idle for long periods may fail sooner, so regular testing is important.

    Water should discharge away from the foundation to a safe area on your property. Many cities prohibit sump discharge into the sanitary sewer.

    Strange noises, constant running, failure to turn on and visible rust are common signs. Testing your pump before the rainy season helps catch problems early.

    Signs you might need a new sump pump include unusual noises during operation, frequent cycling, visible rust or corrosion, or a pump that runs continuously without much water.
