    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Martinsburg, WV

    Tree roots are a leading cause of sewer line damage, especially with older, more porous pipe materials like clay or cast iron. Our sewer replacement services specifically address root-damaged pipes, often recommending more resistant materials like PVC or ABS to stop future intrusions.
