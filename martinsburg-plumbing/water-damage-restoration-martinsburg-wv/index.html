    Musty odors, bubbling paint, warped baseboards and soft spots in floors are typical signs. Stains on ceilings and walls also point to hidden leaks. A rising water bill can signal a leak you have not found yet.

    Most homes dry in three to five days with commercial dehumidifiers and air movers. Dense materials like hardwood or concrete slab may take longer. We monitor moisture daily until everything reaches safe levels.

    Many policies cover sudden and accidental damage, such as a burst pipe or failed water heater. Gradual leaks, poor maintenance and flooding from outside water are often excluded. Check your policy and contact your insurer right away.
