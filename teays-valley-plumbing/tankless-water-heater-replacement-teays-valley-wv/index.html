    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Teays Valley

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by recovering heat from exhaust gases. They vent through low-cost PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Frequent freeze and thaw cycles in the mountains stress fittings and crack exposed pipes over time. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes.
