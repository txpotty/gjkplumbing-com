    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Teays Valley, WV

    PEX and HDPE are flexible, which means fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. HDPE is especially durable for long runs and resists cracking in expansive clay. We use approved compression or fusion fittings and add tracer wire so the line can be found with a locator later. PEX must stay out of sunlight before burial, so we store and lay it carefully on every job site.

    Commercial Water Service Installation in Teays Valley

    Restaurants, retail suites and small commercial buildings need service lines sized for higher demand and often require backflow protection at the meter.     New Construction Water Service Lines in Teays Valley

    New homes, guest houses and additions need a properly sized service line from the meter to the building. We coordinate the rough-in timing with your builder so the line is ready for inspection.
