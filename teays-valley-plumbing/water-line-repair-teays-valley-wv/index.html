    Main Water Line Repair in Teays Valley

    The main water line brings water from the municipal supply to your home. When this line leaks or breaks, it can cause significant water loss, foundation damage, and low water pressure. Our team specializes in diagnosing and repairing main water line issues, from pinpoint leaks to full pipe ruptures, so your home's primary water source is secure. We use advanced detection methods to pinpoint the exact location of the problem, minimizing excavation and disruption to your property in Teays Valley.     Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Teays Valley

    Underground water line leaks are often hard to detect until significant damage has occurred. Common signs include sudden spikes in your water bill, damp spots in your yard, or a clear drop in water pressure. Our team uses acoustic leak detection and ground-penetrating radar to precisely locate these hidden leaks in Teays Valley, reducing the need for extensive excavation.     Water Pressure Restoration in Teays Valley

    Low water pressure can be a frustrating symptom of an underlying water line problem, such as blockages, leaks, or a failing pressure regulator.     Main Water Line Leak Repair in Teays Valley

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We verify the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to locate the exact spot. A single section is cut out and replaced with repair couplings or a new length of pipe, and the line is pressure tested before backfill.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Teays Valley

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process typically takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a long manufacturer warranty.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Teays Valley, WV

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Water hardness in West Virginia ranges from moderate to hard in many areas, and mineral scale is a common issue for water heaters and fixtures. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which chokes flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and ends repeat leaks.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Teays Valley, WV

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant option more and more used in homes across Teays Valley, WV. While PEX is less prone to leaks than older materials, damage can still occur from improper installation, rodent chewing, or impact. Our team is fully experienced in PEX repair, using specialized crimping tools and fittings to make strong, leak-proof connections. We make sure your PEX system continues to perform optimally.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Teays Valley

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off fast in an emergency.
