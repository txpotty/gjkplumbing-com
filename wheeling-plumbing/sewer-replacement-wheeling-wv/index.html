    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Wheeling

    For significant damage or older, failing systems, traditional sewer line replacement often involves excavating a trench to access and replace the entire pipe. This method is reliable for replacing severely collapsed or extensively rooted lines, especially common in older Wheeling homes with cast iron or clay pipes. We thoughtfully plan the excavation to minimize disruption to your yard and landscaping, using heavy-duty equipment for efficient digging and backfilling.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Wheeling

    Cast iron sewer pipe can corrode from the inside, creating rough surfaces and holes. Scale buildup catches debris and causes clogs. We replace cast iron with PVC or HDPE. This restores flow and eliminates corrosion. Many homes built before 1975 have cast iron lines.

    Advanced Diagnostics for Sewer Issues in Wheeling, WV

    Before any replacement begins, an accurate diagnosis is critical. We use advanced sewer camera inspections to get a clear, real-time view of your underground pipes. This allows us to identify the exact location and nature of the damage, whether it's a crack, collapse, severe blockage, or root intrusion. This diagnostic precision ensures that our sewer replacement plan is tailored specifically to your home's needs in Wheeling, WV, avoiding unnecessary work and ensuring a lasting solution.

    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Wheeling, WV

    Tree roots are a common cause of sewer line damage, especially with older, more porous pipe materials like clay or cast iron. Roots can penetrate tiny cracks, grow inside the pipe, and eventually cause blockages or even burst the line. Our sewer replacement services specifically address root-damaged pipes, often recommending more resistant materials like PVC or ABS to stop future intrusions. We also advise on tree placement to protect your new sewer system.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Wheeling

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old line while breaking the old pipe apart. It requires only small access pits at each end. This protects landscaping, driveways and patios. It works well for clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe. Most jobs are finished in one day.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Wheeling, WV

    A cleanout provides simple access for future cleaning and inspection. Many older homes lack an exterior cleanout. We install one during sewer replacement. This saves time and money on later maintenance. It also helps in emergencies.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Wheeling, WV

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the right option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench once it passes inspection. This method allows full correction of slope issues.
