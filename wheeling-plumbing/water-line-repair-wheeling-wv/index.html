    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Wheeling

    For some water line repairs, traditional trenching can be avoided. Trenchless repair methods, like pipe bursting or pipe lining, allow us to fix or replace underground pipes with minimal disruption to your landscaping, driveway, or foundation. This method is often faster and less intrusive than conventional digging, making it a popular choice for homeowners in Wheeling looking to preserve their property. We'll assess whether your water line issue is a good candidate for trenchless technology.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Wheeling, WV

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a simple spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades. We show you the condition of the pipe we expose so you can make the call with actual information.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Wheeling

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off quickly in an emergency.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Wheeling

    Whether your home has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our technicians are experienced in repairing both. PEX offers flexibility and corrosion resistance, while copper is known for its durability. We carry a full stock of matching fittings and tools for each material, ensuring a proper, code-compliant repair. Our expertise extends to identifying common failure points for each type, such as fitting issues with PEX or pinhole leaks in older copper lines, frequent in homes with harder water.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Wheeling, WV

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant option more and more used in homes across Wheeling, WV. While PEX is less prone to leaks than older materials, damage can still occur from improper installation, rodent chewing, or impact. Our team is fully experienced in PEX repair, using specialized crimping tools and fittings to make solid, leak-proof connections. We ensure your PEX system continues to perform optimally.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Wheeling

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process typically takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a long manufacturer warranty.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Wheeling

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We verify the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to locate the exact spot. A single section is cut out and replaced with repair couplings or a new length of pipe, and the line is pressure tested before we backfill.
