    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Moundsville

    A properly functioning temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank.     Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Moundsville, WV

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing hard. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank.     Sediment Flushing & Cleaning in Moundsville, WV

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Moundsville, WV

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is vital for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it affects the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's badly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, saving you from a full tank replacement.
