    Faucet repair involves fixing the existing fixture by replacing specific worn or broken internal components like washers, O-rings, or cartridges.     Simple cartridge swaps can be a DIY job if you identify the correct part. Stuck cartridges, corroded valve bodies and leaks under the sink are best handled by a plumber.
