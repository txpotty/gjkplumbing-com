    While no pipe is completely freeze-proof, materials like PEX, or cross-linked polyethylene, are more flexible and less likely to suffer bursting when frozen compared to rigid copper or PVC.     A frost-free hose bib has a long stem that shuts off water inside the warm part of the wall. Brands like Woodford and Prier make dependable models. They greatly reduce freeze damage at outdoor faucets.

    Most thawing jobs take one to two hours. Burst pipe repairs typically take one to four hours depending on access. Larger freeze damage with multiple splits can take a full day.

    While any pipe can freeze, older galvanized steel or copper pipes in uninsulated areas are often more susceptible to bursting due to their rigid material.
