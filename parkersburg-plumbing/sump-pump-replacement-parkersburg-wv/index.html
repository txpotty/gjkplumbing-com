    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Parkersburg

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Parkersburg for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, appropriately sized pump. We make sure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and maximize efficiency. That is crucial for handling heavy rainfall.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Parkersburg

    Power outages during severe storms can leave a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump offers crucial protection during these times. We install and replace complete battery backup systems, including dedicated marine-grade batteries and smart charging units. Our team helps you select a system with enough run-time to protect your Parkersburg home through extended power interruptions. You get peace of mind when the lights go out.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Parkersburg, WV

    Zoeller pumps are known for their reliability and heavy-duty construction, making them a top choice for many Parkersburg, WV homeowners. GJK Plumbing specializes in Zoeller sump pump replacement, so you get a quality, long-lasting unit.     Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Parkersburg

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Parkersburg, WV

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more long-lasting. We provide professional pedestal sump pump replacement in Parkersburg, WV, with proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise is not a major concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Parkersburg, WV

    For maximum protection, many homeowners in Parkersburg choose a combination sump pump system, which pairs a primary electric pump with a battery backup pump in one unit. This keeps the system running even if the power fails or the primary pump malfunctions. GJK Plumbing can replace your existing combination system or install a new one, evaluating your needs for groundwater management. We make sure the components are properly matched for strong performance and longevity.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Parkersburg, WV

    Some properties use pumps in yard drainage pits or below-grade window wells to handle storm runoff. These pumps may sit idle for months, then fail when they are needed most. We replace them with corrosion-resistant models and clear debris from the pit. We can route discharge away from foundations and onto approved areas. Regular testing before storm season is a good habit.

    Commercial Pit Pump Replacement in Parkersburg
