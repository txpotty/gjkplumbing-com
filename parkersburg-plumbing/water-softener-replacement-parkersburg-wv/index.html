    Traditional Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Parkersburg

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and effective solution for hard water in Parkersburg.     Metered Softener Replacement in Parkersburg

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly used up. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first month.

    Requirements vary by city. Simple replacements may not require one, but new plumbing connections may. We check local rules for you.

    Salt-based softeners remove hardness minerals, calcium and magnesium, through ion exchange, truly softening the water. Salt-free conditioners do not remove minerals but change their crystal structure so they do not stick to surfaces. That prevents scale buildup without actually softening the water.

    Water hardness in West Virginia ranges from moderate to hard in many areas, and mineral scale is a common issue for water heaters and fixtures. Hard water contains dissolved minerals like calcium and magnesium. These minerals can lead to scale buildup in pipes, reduced efficiency of water heaters and appliances, spots on dishes, and dry skin and hair. A working water softener is essential for combating these issues in Parkersburg homes.

    To extend the life of your new water softener, use the correct type of salt, if applicable, and keep the brine tank filled. Regular maintenance, such as cleaning the brine tank and periodic inspections by a professional, can also help ensure its longevity and efficiency.
