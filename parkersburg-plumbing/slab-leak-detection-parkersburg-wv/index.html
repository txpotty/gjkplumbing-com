    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Parkersburg, WV

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to see temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a distinct thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area significantly without any physical disruption to your property in Parkersburg.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Parkersburg, WV

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are harder to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know where to listen.

    Electronic Leak Detection in Parkersburg

    For hard-to-find leaks, our electronic leak detection equipment sends a signal through your pipes and measures changes that indicate a breach. This technology allows us to locate leaks in various pipe materials, including copper, PEX and PVC, commonly found in Parkersburg homes. It helps prevent exploratory digging, saving you time and money.

    Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection in Parkersburg

    Our team uses electronic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a slab leak. These highly sensitive microphones can detect the faint sound of water escaping from a pressurized pipe under your foundation. This non-invasive method helps us avoid unnecessary concrete breaking, saving you time and money on repairs. It's especially effective for copper and PEX lines commonly found in Parkersburg homes.
