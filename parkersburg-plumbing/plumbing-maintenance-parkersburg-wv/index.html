    Aging galvanized pipe narrows with rust and scale over time, causing low water pressure, leaks and discolored water.     Regular water heater maintenance is crucial to preventing sediment buildup. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements.     Water Softener Maintenance in Parkersburg

    Water softeners combat hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Proper softener maintenance prolongs its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing expensive scale damage.
