    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Weirton

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Weirton. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. GJK Plumbing focuses on precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with trusted brands like Watts and Febco.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Weirton

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a common backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Weirton, DCVAs are often installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers precisely install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.
