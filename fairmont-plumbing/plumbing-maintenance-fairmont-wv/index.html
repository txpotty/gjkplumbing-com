    Routine Plumbing Maintenance Services in Fairmont, WV

    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Fairmont

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get swapped out. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility first.

    Repiping and Polybutylene Replacement in Fairmont, WV

    Homes with polybutylene or aging galvanized pipe face a higher risk of leaks and low water pressure. During maintenance, we check pipe materials and look for signs of failure. If repiping makes sense, we explain PEX and copper options and give a written estimate. Repiping can be done in stages to fit your budget. New pipe brings back strong pressure and peace of mind.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Fairmont

    Regular water heater maintenance is crucial to preventing sediment buildup. Many West Virginia homes rely on private wells or springs, and that water can carry iron, manganese or sulfur that discolors fixtures and laundry. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, boosts efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Fairmont

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers call for annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where necessary. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Fairmont

    Manufactured homes are common in rural West Virginia, and their plumbing often needs extra attention to skirting, insulation and freeze protection. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are common signs. Finding a leak early prevents structural damage and mold.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Fairmont, WV

    Flash flooding is a serious risk in West Virginia, where heavy rain moves quickly down steep slopes into narrow valleys and creeks. A working sump pump is essential for homes with basements or crawl spaces when heavy rain overwhelms drainage. Our maintenance includes testing the float switch, checking for proper discharge, cleaning the pit of debris, and inspecting the check valve. That way your sump pump is ready to protect your home from flooding when unexpected storms hit Fairmont.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Fairmont

    Tankless water heaters need specialized maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is especially important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and potential breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.

    Drain Line Inspections for Maintenance in Fairmont

    As part of complete plumbing maintenance, we offer drain line camera inspections. This lets us see the condition of your main sewer lines, spotting potential blockages from roots or grease buildup, or structural issues like cracks and offsets made worse by shifting soil. Rocky, steep terrain is common across West Virginia, and many homes sit on hillsides where drainage and slope movement affect buried lines. Early detection through inspection can prevent severe backups and costly emergency repairs.
