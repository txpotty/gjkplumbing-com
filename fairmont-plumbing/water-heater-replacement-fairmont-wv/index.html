    Choosing the right size depends on the number of people in your household, their hot water usage habits and peak demand times.     Most replacements take two to four hours. Attic installs or relocations can take longer.

    Flushing a traditional tank water heater once a year is recommended in most homes. This helps remove sediment buildup, which can reduce efficiency and shorten the lifespan of the unit. Regular flushing can also prevent scale from damaging heating elements.

    While it might seem tempting, water heater replacement involves gas lines, high voltage electricity, water pressure and heavy equipment.
