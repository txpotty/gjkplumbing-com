    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can build one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, hardpan or rock and set a durable basin.     High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in South Charleston

    A high water alarm lets you know when water rises above normal levels. Smart monitors can send alerts to your phone when the pump runs or fails.
