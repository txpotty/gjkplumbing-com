    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in South Charleston

    Leaks in underground water lines, from your meter to your home, can go unnoticed for long periods, wasting water and causing yard erosion. GJK Plumbing uses ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection methods to accurately map out and pinpoint leaks in buried pipes, allowing for targeted excavation and repair rather than digging up your entire yard.

    Leak Detection for PEX and Copper Pipes in South Charleston

    Whether your plumbing system uses modern PEX tubing or traditional copper pipes, GJK Plumbing has the expertise to detect leaks in both materials. Our tools are effective across many pipe materials, allowing accurate detection regardless of your home's plumbing type.

    Water Meter Leak Testing in South Charleston

    The water meter is the first and easiest leak test. With every fixture and appliance off, a moving leak indicator means water is escaping somewhere. We read the flow rate to estimate how many gallons are being lost. Then we shut off zones one at a time to see which part of the property is losing water.

    Pool Water Leak Detection in South Charleston, WV

    Pools lose a lot of water to evaporation in summer heat, so a leak can hide behind normal loss. We use a bucket test to compare evaporation with actual water loss. Then we pressure test pool plumbing and dye test fittings, lights and skimmers. Finding the leak protects the pool and the soil around it.
