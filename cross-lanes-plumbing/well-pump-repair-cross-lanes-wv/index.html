    Pressure Switch Replacement in Cross Lanes

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are frequent causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Cross Lanes, WV

    Variable speed systems like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by adjusting motor speed. When they fault out, the drive usually shows a code that guides diagnosis. We troubleshoot drives, sensors and wiring and repair or replace components. These systems need a smaller pressure tank but careful setup.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Cross Lanes, WV

    Jet pumps, typically found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. GJK Plumbing can reliably troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Cross Lanes operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is essential for jet pump function.

    Well Pump Pressure Tank Issues in Cross Lanes

    The pressure tank works together with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. A waterlogged tank, a ruptured diaphragm, or a faulty air valve can cause rapid pump cycling, fluctuating water pressure, or even no water at all. Our team carefully diagnoses pressure tank problems and performs necessary repairs or replacements. We ensure your tank is properly sized and charged for optimal system performance in Cross Lanes.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Cross Lanes, WV

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Snowmelt combined with spring rain can raise rivers and groundwater, putting extra pressure on foundations and drainage systems. Lightning and power surges frequently damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help prevent repeat failures.
