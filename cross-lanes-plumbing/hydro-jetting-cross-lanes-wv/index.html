    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Cross Lanes

    Residential hydro jetting in Cross Lanes typically costs $350 to $800, depending on line length, access and how heavy the buildup is. Commercial jetting is often priced by the hour and may run higher.

    A thorough jetting often keeps a line clear for a year or more, depending on use and roots. Lines with heavy root intrusion may need maintenance or a repair.

    Small consumer jetters exist, but they lack the pressure and flow of professional units. High-pressure water can also injure untrained users and damage fragile pipe.

    We use professional cart and trailer jetters along with rotating and root-cutting nozzles from established manufacturers. Color camera systems record the line before and after.

    Key signs you might need hydro jetting include frequently recurring clogs in the same drain, multiple slow drains throughout your home, persistent foul odors coming from drains, or gurgling sounds from toilets or drains. These often point to a significant buildup or blockage that needs more than just a snake.

    While hydro jetting is very powerful, it is usually safe for most sound plumbing systems, including PVC, cast iron, and concrete pipes. However, it is not recommended for severely damaged, corroded, or fragile pipes, which is why a pre-jetting camera inspection by GJK Plumbing is essential to assess pipe integrity and prevent further damage.

    GJK Plumbing uses professional-grade hydro jetting equipment from trusted manufacturers like Spartan Tool and MyTana. These brands are known for their reliability, powerful performance, and safety features, so we can deliver the most thorough cleaning for your pipes.

    Repeat clogs in the same line, slow drains after snaking, sewer odors and known grease or root problems are good reasons to consider jetting.
