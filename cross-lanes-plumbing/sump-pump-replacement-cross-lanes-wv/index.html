    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Cross Lanes

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Cross Lanes

    A standard sump pump replacement typically costs $500 to $1,200 installed. Battery backup systems usually add $800 to $1,800. Sewage ejector pumps usually cost $1,200 to $2,500.

    Most sump pump replacements take one to two hours. Battery backups or new discharge lines take a bit longer.

    While it might seem straightforward, professional sump pump replacement is highly recommended. It involves electrical connections, proper plumbing for the discharge line, and making sure the pump is correctly sized and installed to prevent future flooding and comply with local Cross Lanes codes.

    Constant running, loud grinding, frequent cycling or failure to start are typical signs. Rust, age and a burning smell also indicate trouble.

    To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and make sure the discharge pipe is clear. An annual professional inspection by GJK Plumbing can also help lengthen its lifespan.

    It is a good idea to test your sump pump at least every three months, and especially before and after rainy seasons. To test, simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit. The pump should activate, drain the water and then shut off automatically. If it does not, it is time for a professional inspection.
