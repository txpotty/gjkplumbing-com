    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Cross Lanes, WV

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates suction to draw brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. When it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying. We clean or replace the injector and screen and check the brine line for leaks.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Cross Lanes

    A common issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to poor regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and making sure the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering precise repairs or replacements.
