    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Buckhannon, WV

    A salt bridge, a crust of salt that forms in the brine tank, can prevent your water softener from regenerating properly. This leads to hard water throughout your home. Our team can safely break up and remove salt bridges, clean the brine tank, and advise on best practices to prevent future occurrences. We also repair leaks or damage to the brine tank itself, frequent in older systems.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Buckhannon, WV

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates suction to draw brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. When it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying. We clean or replace the injector and screen and check the brine line for air leaks.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Buckhannon, WV

    Leaks in a water softener can range from minor drips around fittings to significant puddles from a cracked tank or faulty valve. These leaks can cause water damage and waste water. Our plumbers quickly pinpoint the source of leaks, whether from seals, O-rings, hoses, or internal components, and perform lasting repairs. We use appropriate sealing compounds and replacement parts for a lasting fix in Buckhannon, WV.

    Resin Bed Replacement & Regeneration Issues in Buckhannon

    Over time, the resin beads inside your water softener can become fouled, damaged, or depleted, reducing their ability to remove hardness minerals. If your system is regenerating but still delivering hard water, the resin bed may be the culprit. We can test the resin, flush the system to remove iron or sediment buildup, or perform a complete resin replacement. This process revitalizes your softener's core function, ensuring effective hard water treatment for years to come.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Buckhannon, WV

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, regulating the flow of water and initiating regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water, regenerate too often, or leak. We diagnose issues like stuck pistons, worn out seals, motor failures, or programming errors. Our plumbers carry a range of replacement parts for popular brands like Fleck, Clack, and Autotrol, allowing a quick and effective repair to restore proper operation.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Buckhannon, WV

    While less common than traditional softeners, no-salt water conditioners also require maintenance and periodic repair. These systems work by conditioning water to prevent scale buildup without removing minerals. If you notice scale returning or reduced water pressure, your no-salt system may need attention. GJK Plumbing is experienced in diagnosing and repairing issues with these specialized units in Buckhannon, WV, ensuring they continue to protect your plumbing and appliances effectively.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Buckhannon

Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and ensuring the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly.
