    A slab leak is a leak in the water pipes embedded within or beneath your home's concrete slab foundation.     Older homes may have galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes, both known for higher leak rates due to corrosion or material degradation. Copper pipes can develop pinhole leaks over time, while PVC and PEX are usually more reliable but can still leak at fittings or due to installation errors.

    To prevent future leaks, consider regular plumbing inspections, especially for older homes. Keep water pressure in a safe range, address any signs of corrosion and maintain your plumbing system. For homes with known issues, proactive repiping or pipe upgrades can be a long-term solution.
