    Damaged Baffle Repair in Brookhaven

    The baffles inside a grease trap are critical for separating FOGs from wastewater. If these baffles are corroded, broken, or dislodged, the trap's efficiency plummets, allowing grease into the sewer system. Our service includes inspecting the condition of all baffles and repairing or replacing them as needed, often using durable, corrosion-resistant materials. Proper baffle function is key to compliance with wastewater regulations in Brookhaven.

    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Brookhaven

    Grease traps often clog due to excessive accumulation of fats, oils, and grease (FOGs) or food solids. Our technicians use high-pressure hydro-jetting to break down tough blockages and thoroughly clean the internal baffles and lines. We also inspect for underlying issues like damaged pipes or improper disposal practices that contribute to repeat clogs, offering solutions to prevent future blockages. Identifying the exact location and nature of the clog is key for effective repair and long-term performance in your Brookhaven establishment.

    Grease Trap Leak Repair in Brookhaven

    Leaks can come from cracked trap bodies, failed gaskets or loose pipe connections. Indoor leaks can damage floors and create slip hazards in the kitchen. We locate the source and replace gaskets, seal fittings or repair cracks when possible. Outdoor leaks can contaminate soil and lead to odors around the building. If the body is badly damaged, we suggest replacement.

    Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Brookhaven

    Beyond the trap itself, the drain lines leading to and from the grease trap can also suffer damage or blockages. These lines are particularly susceptible to grease build-up. In Brookhaven, we diagnose and repair issues within these connecting pipes, including clearing FOG accumulation, fixing collapsed sections, or repairing root intrusions. Ensuring these lines are clear is vital for the overall efficiency of your grease trap system.

Correct flow control is necessary for a trap to pass inspection.
