    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in Bridgeport

    Polybutylene's built-in weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Testing well water every year or so is a good habit in West Virginia, especially after flooding or nearby ground disturbance. Hard water can also add stress to pipe that is already breaking down. Understanding this failure mechanism is important for diagnosing issues and recommending the most effective and permanent replacement strategy for your home.
