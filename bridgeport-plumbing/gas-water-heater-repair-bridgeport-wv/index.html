    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Bridgeport

    Strange noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem. Hard water can speed up sediment accumulation. Our repair service includes flushing the tank and inspecting for sediment, addressing pressure issues or checking other components that might be causing the noise, restoring efficiency and quiet operation.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Bridgeport, WV

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame points to a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed. A clean blue flame heats more quickly and produces less carbon monoxide. We also check for rust flakes dropping from the flue.

    Restoring Hot Water for Older Homes in Bridgeport

    Many homes in Bridgeport have older gas water heater models that require specific knowledge for repair.     Pilot Light Repair in Bridgeport

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and clean or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.

    Thermostat and Gas Valve Repair in Bridgeport, WV

    If your gas water heater isn't heating water to the correct temperature, or if it is overheating, the thermostat or gas valve might be to blame. These components regulate the gas supply and temperature. Our plumbers in Bridgeport, WV can test the thermostat for accuracy and inspect the gas valve for any blockages or faults. We carry a range of replacement parts for various brands, allowing for fast and efficient repairs.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Bridgeport, WV

    The gas control valve regulates temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something fails. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or listed universal model. We leak test and confirm temperature control afterward.

    Pilot Light and Ignition System Repair in Bridgeport

    A frequent culprit for no hot water in gas heaters is a faulty pilot light or ignition system. Our technicians are experienced in diagnosing issues like a dirty pilot tube, thermocouple failure or a bad igniter. We carry common replacement parts on our service vehicles to ensure a quick fix and restore your hot water supply promptly.
