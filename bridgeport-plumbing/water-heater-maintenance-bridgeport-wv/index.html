    Gas water heater maintenance involves crucial safety inspections in Bridgeport, WV. We check for gas leaks, proper venting of exhaust gases, and the function of the pilot light or igniter.     Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Bridgeport

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part in your water heater designed to corrode instead of the tank itself. Depending on your water chemistry, these rods can wear down faster than expected. During maintenance, we inspect the anode rod's condition and recommend replacement if necessary. A healthy anode rod is crucial for preventing rust and extending the lifespan of your water heater tank, saving you from a costly full replacement.

    Standard Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Bridgeport

    Our standard tank water heater maintenance includes flushing the tank to remove sediment buildup. Water hardness in West Virginia ranges from moderate to hard in many areas, and mineral scale is a common issue for water heaters and fixtures. We also inspect the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and check for any leaks or corrosion on the tank and connections.     T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Bridgeport, WV

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building unsafe pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets swapped out. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge if you have one.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Bridgeport, WV

    Tankless water heaters need specific maintenance, mainly a descaling flush to remove mineral deposits that can restrict flow and reduce efficiency. Our technicians in Bridgeport, WV use commercial-grade solutions to clean the heat exchanger, so your unit keeps providing on-demand hot water efficiently. We also inspect the igniter, fan, and exhaust venting for optimal performance and safety.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Bridgeport, WV

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as necessary. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water quickly. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.
