    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in St. Albans

    Most PEX repipes for West Virginia homes cost $5,000 to $12,000, depending on size, bathrooms and access. Copper repipes usually run 20 to 40 percent more.

    Most homes are repiped in two to four days, plus a day for drywall patching. Water is typically restored each evening.

    We primarily install quality copper piping from reputable manufacturers like Mueller Streamline and Cerro, known for their durability and purity. For PEX systems, we often use leading brands such as Uponor (Wirsbo Aquapex), Zurn PEX, or Viega PEX, chosen for their proven reliability and performance in homes.

    Common signs you need repiping include persistent low water pressure throughout your home, discolored or rusty-looking water, frequent pipe leaks, visible corrosion on exposed pipes, and a metallic taste or odor in your water. If your home is over 50 years old with original pipes, it's a good idea to have them inspected, especially if they are galvanized or polybutylene.

    Copper is a rigid, durable metal with a long lifespan, known for its resistance to bacteria. PEX is a flexible plastic tubing that is more affordable, easier to install, resistant to freezing and corrosion, and very quiet. Both are strong choices for repiping, and our specialists can help you decide which material is best suited for your home and budget in St. Albans, WV.

    While it's possible to repipe only a section of your home, we often recommend a whole-home repipe if your entire system is aging or made of problematic materials. Sectional repiping can be a temporary fix if other parts of your plumbing are still vulnerable. Our specialists can assess your situation and advise on the most cost-effective and long-term solution for your St. Albans home.

    PEX resists scale and pinhole corrosion and handles expansion well, which makes it a strong choice for hard water. Copper is durable too but costs more and can pinhole in corrosive water.

    We provide a written workmanship warranty, and PEX manufacturers such as Uponor offer long-term warranties when certified installers use their system. We explain the details before work begins.
