    Sewer Pipe Relining in Nitro, WV

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an excellent option for preserving landscapes in Nitro.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Nitro, WV

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. Shale and clay soils can hold water and move over time, which can crack sewer lines and push water into basements. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Nitro

    Every sewer repair starts with a look inside the pipe. We run a self-leveling camera from your cleanout or a pulled toilet and record the full length of the line. A sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact location and depth of every problem on the surface. You get a copy of the video and a plain-language summary of what we found. This step alone frequently saves homeowners from paying for repairs they do not need.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Nitro

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to recurring clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it holds.
