    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Nitro, WV

    Pipe bursting is another trenchless method used for replacing severely damaged pipes. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is excellent for completely replacing a pipe segment with a new, stronger material, such as HDPE. It's often chosen when the existing pipe is collapsed or too damaged for lining. GJK Plumbing uses specialized bursting heads and winches for a smooth and efficient replacement, reducing the overall project time and cost for homeowners in Nitro, WV.
