    Frequently Asked Questions About Drains Camera Inspection in Ranson

    A standard drain or sewer camera inspection usually costs $150 to $400. Adding locating and depth marking usually brings the total to $250 to $500. If we clear the line first, cleaning is billed separately.

    We use professional-grade, high-resolution waterproof sewer cameras from trusted brands like Wohler, MyTana, and Ridgid. These cameras are equipped with powerful LED lights for clear visibility and often include self-leveling heads to maintain an upright image, for reliable diagnostics.

    While mainly for inspecting the inside of pipes, many of our drain cameras are equipped with sondes (transmitters) that allow us to precisely locate the camera's location underground. This can be very useful for locating septic tanks, leach fields, or hidden cleanouts when their exact position is unknown on your property in Ranson.

    Most residential drain camera inspections take between 30 minutes and 1 hour, depending on the length and complexity of the pipe system. More extensive sewer line inspections or those involving multiple access points might take slightly longer, up to 2 hours.

    Inexpensive consumer cameras exist, but they lack the range, locating ability and image quality needed to judge pipe condition. Interpreting the footage also takes experience. A professional inspection gives reliable results.

    For most homes, a drain camera inspection every 5-7 years is a good preventative measure, especially for older properties or if you have mature trees nearby. If you experience frequent clogs or are planning major renovations, annual or biennial inspections might be beneficial to catch problems early.

    If the camera identifies a blockage, we will discuss the best course of action. This might involve hydro-jetting to clear grease and debris, snaking to remove roots or foreign objects, or in severe cases, recommending pipe repair or replacement. The camera helps us choose the most effective solution.
