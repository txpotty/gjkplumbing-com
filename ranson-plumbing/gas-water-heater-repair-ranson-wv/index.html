    Pilot Light Repair in Ranson

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most frequent gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and clean or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.

    Pilot Light and Ignition System Repair in Ranson

    A frequent culprit for no hot water in gas heaters is a faulty pilot light or ignition system. We carry common replacement parts on our service vehicles to ensure a quick fix and restore your hot water flow promptly.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Ranson

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it detects vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and trip the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as needed.

    Pilot Light and Burner Issues in Ranson

    A common problem with gas water heaters is a pilot light that won't stay lit or a burner that fails to ignite. This can be caused by a faulty thermocouple, a dirty pilot tube or issues with the gas control valve. Our technicians in Ranson are skilled at diagnosing these components and performing the needed repairs, whether it is cleaning, adjusting or replacing parts.     Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Ranson

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures often involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the failure. Correct vent slope and termination are checked before we leave.

    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Ranson

    Odd noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem. Hard water can add to sediment accumulation. Our repair service includes flushing the tank and inspecting for sediment, addressing pressure issues or checking other components that might be causing the noise, improving efficiency and quiet operation.
