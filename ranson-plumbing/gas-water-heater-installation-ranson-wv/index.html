    Power vent units use a fan to push exhaust outside, while direct vent units use a sealed system that draws outside air. Both allow more flexible placement compared to atmospheric vents.

    It's recommended to have your gas water heater professionally serviced once a year. Regular maintenance can extend its lifespan, improve efficiency and catch potential issues before they become major problems, ensuring safe operation.

    A conventional gas water heater stores and heats a large volume of water in a tank, providing a limited supply. A gas tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit, offering an endless supply but requiring more complex venting and gas line work.
