    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Morgantown, WV

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Testing well water every year or so is a good habit in West Virginia, especially after flooding or nearby ground disturbance. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which chokes flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and ends repeat leaks.
