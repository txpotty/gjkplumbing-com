    Pilot Light Repair in Morgantown

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.
