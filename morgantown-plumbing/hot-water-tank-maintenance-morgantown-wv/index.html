    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Morgantown, WV

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition.     Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Morgantown, WV

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing hard. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank.     Electric Hot Water Tank Element Check in Morgantown

    Electric hot water tanks rely on heating elements to warm the water. During maintenance, our plumbers inspect these elements for signs of wear, corrosion or burnout.     Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Morgantown, WV

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can clog the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a stable blue flame and lower gas bills.
