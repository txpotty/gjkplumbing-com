    Pilot Light Repair in Vienna

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.

    Emergency Gas Water Heater Repair in Vienna

    When your gas water heater quits working, especially on a busy morning, it is an emergency. GJK Plumbing offers prompt emergency repair services for gas water heaters in Vienna. We prioritize urgent calls to get your hot water back quickly, understanding that a lack of hot water is more than just an inconvenience.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Vienna

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it detects vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and shut down the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as needed.

    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Vienna

    Strange noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem. Hard water can add to sediment accumulation. Our repair service includes flushing the tank and inspecting for sediment, addressing pressure issues or checking other components that might be causing the noise, restoring efficiency and quiet operation.

    Water Heater Tank Leaks and Pressure Issues in Vienna, WV

    While gas water heaters are sturdy, tank leaks or pressure problems can occur. We can often fix small fitting leaks or replace relief valves if pressure is too high. If the tank itself is compromised due to rust or old age, a replacement might be the more sensible long-term solution. We will provide an honest assessment of the repair options for your Vienna, WV home.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Vienna, WV

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame points to a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed. A clean blue flame heats faster and produces less carbon monoxide. We also check for rust flakes falling from the flue.

    All Major Gas Water Heater Brands Serviced in Vienna

    Whether you have a Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith or another major brand of gas water heater, our technicians are trained to service them all. We have access to a wide range of parts and the right diagnostic tools needed for various models. That means your repair is done correctly the first time, regardless of the brand.
