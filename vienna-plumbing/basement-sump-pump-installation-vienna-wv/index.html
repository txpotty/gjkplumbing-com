    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Vienna

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. They usually last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we usually recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod adjusted to your pit depth.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Vienna, WV

    Power outages can happen, especially during severe weather, and they leave your primary sump pump useless. We supply and install battery backup sump pump systems that automatically activate if the power goes out, providing continuous protection for your basement in Vienna, WV. Flooded basements and sewer backups are frequent after major storms, so backwater valves and sump pumps are worthwhile upgrades. These systems include a deep cycle battery and a secondary pump, so your basement stays dry even when the electricity fails.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Vienna

    Watch for a pump that runs constantly, cycles on and off rapidly, makes grinding noises or never seems to turn on. Rust stains, a stuck float and water sitting above the switch level are also red flags.

    Manufacturer warranties typically range from 3 to 5 years on quality cast iron pumps. We also back our installation labor, and we will walk you through the specific coverage for the model you choose.

    It is a good idea to test your sump pump every 2 to 3 months, especially before and after the rainy season. Simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit to make sure the pump activates, drains the water and shuts off properly. Regular testing helps identify issues early.

    Most residential basements need a 1/3 or 1/2 horsepower pump, but the right answer depends on how fast water enters the pit and how high it must be lifted. We measure both before recommending a model.
