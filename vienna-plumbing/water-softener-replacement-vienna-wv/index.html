    Long-Lasting Water Softener Replacement Services in Vienna, WV

    Brine Tank Replacement in Vienna

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore consistent brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the right salt type for your system.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Alternatives in Vienna, WV

    Some homeowners prefer a salt-free scale reduction system that uses template-assisted crystallization media. These systems do not remove hardness minerals but change them so they are less likely to stick. They require no drain and no salt. We explain the differences honestly, since they work differently than true softeners. They can be a good fit when drain access is limited or sodium is a concern.

    Softener Loop Installation in Vienna, WV

    Many homes have a softener loop near the main water line that makes for easy installation. When a home lacks one, we can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. We also route a drain line to a laundry box, floor drain or approved location. A good loop keeps outdoor hose bibs on hard water to save salt. This makes future service simpler.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Vienna, WV

    Fleck is a well-known brand for reliable water softener control valves, found in many residential and commercial systems across West Virginia. If your Fleck-controlled softener is beyond repair or simply outdated, GJK Plumbing can expertly replace the entire unit. We know many Fleck models and can recommend an equivalent or upgraded system that fits cleanly with your existing plumbing in Vienna.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Vienna

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly used up. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first month.

    Commercial Water Softener Replacement in Vienna

    Businesses in Vienna often rely on robust water softening systems to protect equipment and maintain product quality.     Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Vienna, WV

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more economical than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes better sense.

    Water Softener Failure Diagnosis in Vienna, WV

    Before replacing, we check whether your softener has a simple fix like a clogged injector, failed motor or wrong settings. If the valve is obsolete or parts are no longer available, replacement is usually the better value. We test hardness before and after the softener to confirm the problem.
