    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Vienna

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with tight space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid without trouble.

    New Restaurant Build-Out Grease Traps in Vienna

    If you are opening a new restaurant, the grease trap is often a key part of plan review. We work with your architect or contractor to size the unit and show it on plumbing plans. We also coordinate with the city's pretreatment staff when they require a review.

    In-Ground Gravity Interceptor Installation in Vienna, WV

    Larger restaurants and high-volume kitchens often need an outdoor gravity interceptor. These concrete or polyethylene tanks typically range from several hundred to over 1,500 gallons. We excavate, set the tank on a stable base, connect inlet and outlet piping and install manhole risers. Shale and clay soils can hold water and move over time, which can crack sewer lines and push water into basements. We plan excavation equipment around the ground conditions we find on site. A properly installed interceptor can go far longer between pump-outs than an indoor trap.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Installation in Vienna

    For high-volume commercial kitchens in Vienna, large outdoor grease interceptors are often required by code.
