    PEX Water Line Installation in Pea Ridge

    PEX, or cross-linked polyethylene, is a popular choice for its flexibility, durability and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Frequent freeze and thaw cycles in the mountains loosen fittings and crack exposed pipes over time. GJK Plumbing professionally installs PEX water lines for both new construction and replacements in Pea Ridge. PEX offers excellent flow rates and is less prone to bursting than traditional rigid pipe, providing a long-lasting, cost-effective solution for your home's plumbing.

    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Pea Ridge, WV

    PEX and HDPE are flexible, which means fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. HDPE is especially durable for long runs and holds up against cracking in expansive clay. We use approved compression or fusion fittings and add tracer wire so the line can be found with a locator later. PEX must stay out of sunlight before burial, so we store and lay it carefully on every job site.
