    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Pea Ridge

    Tankless units are especially sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures consistent.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Pea Ridge, WV

    Gas water heater maintenance involves important safety inspections in Pea Ridge, WV. We check for gas leaks, proper venting of exhaust gases, and the function of the pilot light or igniter.     T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Pea Ridge, WV

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building dangerous pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets replaced. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge where installed.

    Attic and Garage Water Heater Maintenance in Pea Ridge

    Many homes have water heaters in garages or attics, where heat, cold and tight access add stress to the unit. Attic units need working drain pans and drain lines to prevent ceiling damage if the tank leaks. We check the pan, drain line, venting and access platform. Garage units should be raised and protected where required for gas appliances. We confirm your setup meets local code.

    Tank Water Heater Flushing in Pea Ridge

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clean. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs quieter.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Pea Ridge

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part in your water heater designed to corrode instead of the tank itself. Depending on your water chemistry, these rods can wear down faster than expected. During maintenance, we inspect the anode rod's condition and recommend replacement if necessary. A healthy anode rod is crucial for preventing rust and extending the lifespan of your water heater tank, saving you from a costly full replacement.
