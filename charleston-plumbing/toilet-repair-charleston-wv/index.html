    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Charleston, WV

    Water around the base usually means the wax ring has failed or the toilet has been rocking on a loose flange. On slab foundations, the flange often sits on concrete and can crack when bolts are overtightened. We pull the toilet, inspect the flange, install a repair ring or new flange if needed and reset the bowl on a fresh seal. We also check for soft flooring so you know if any water damage needs attention.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Charleston, WV

    A hairline crack in the tank or a bulging braided supply line can let go without warning and flood a bathroom. We inspect the tank, tank-to-bowl bolts and gasket, and the supply line on every visit. If the porcelain is cracked we will price a replacement tank or new toilet, and we upgrade old plastic or vinyl supply lines to braided stainless.

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Charleston, WV

    If your toilet needs two flushes or clogs often, the problem could be scale in the rim jets, a partial blockage in the trapway or a flush valve that does not open fully. We auger the trap, check the vent if gurgling is present and clean the jets with a descaling treatment. Some low-flow toilets from the 1990s simply flush poorly, and we will tell you honestly if replacement is the better fix.

    Toilet Tank and Bowl Crack Repair in Charleston

    Cracks in a toilet tank or bowl in Charleston can lead to serious water leakage and require immediate attention. While small hairline cracks might be temporarily sealed, larger or structural cracks often require replacement of the affected component. Our technicians assess the severity of the crack and advise on the most cost-effective and durable solution. We can perform careful tank or bowl replacements, ensuring a watertight seal and proper function, preventing further water damage to your flooring or subfloor.

    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Charleston

    Pressure-assist toilets from Sloan Flushmate and dual-flush models from TOTO and American Standard use parts that hardware stores seldom stock. We carry cartridges, flush buttons and seals for the common models and can order specialty parts fast. These systems need correct pressure settings to work well, so we test incoming water pressure as part of the repair.

    Toilet Flush Mechanism Repair in Charleston, WV

    If your toilet isn't flushing properly, it could be due to issues with the flush chain, handle, flapper, or fill valve. A weak flush may mean the tank isn't filling correctly, while a loose handle might not be engaging the flapper. Our plumbers are skilled at diagnosing these internal tank problems, replacing worn or broken components like flush valves, fill valves, and toilet handles to restore full and effective flushing power.

    Running Toilet Repair in Charleston

    A running toilet is not just annoying, it can waste hundreds of gallons of water a day, leading to significantly higher water bills. This common issue is usually caused by a faulty flapper, a misaligned fill valve, or an improperly adjusted float. Our plumbers quickly identify the exact cause, whether it's mineral buildup from hard water affecting the flapper seal or a worn-out component, and replace the necessary parts to stop the continuous flow and restore efficiency.
