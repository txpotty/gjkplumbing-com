    Resin Bed Replacement in Charleston

    Over time, the resin beads in your water softener can become fouled or degrade, losing their ability to exchange ions and soften water. This is a common issue, especially with older systems or those exposed to high levels of chlorine. GJK Plumbing provides professional resin bed replacement services, carefully removing old resin and installing new, high-quality media to restore your softener's efficiency and capacity in Charleston.

    Emergency Water Softener Repair in Charleston

    A sudden water softener breakdown can leave your home vulnerable to instant hard water problems, especially if it leads to a leak. We offer emergency water softener repair services to address urgent issues promptly. Whether your system is continuously draining, completely unresponsive, or flooding your utility room, our rapid response team in Charleston is available to diagnose and repair the problem efficiently, limiting disruption and damage to your property.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Charleston

    A frequent issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to inefficient regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and making sure the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering targeted repairs or replacements.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Charleston

    A softener using far too much salt or water often has settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units may regenerate on a schedule regardless of use. We retest hardness, reprogram the valve or upgrade the head to a metered control. Proper settings can noticeably cut salt and water use.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Charleston, WV

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, regulating the flow of water and starting regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water, regenerate too often, or leak. We diagnose issues like stuck pistons, worn out seals, motor failures, or programming errors. Our plumbers carry a range of replacement parts for popular brands like Fleck, Clack, and Autotrol, ensuring a quick and effective repair to restore proper operation.
