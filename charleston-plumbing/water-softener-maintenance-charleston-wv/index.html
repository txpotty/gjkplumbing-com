    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Charleston

    Softener resin can foul with iron, manganese or sediment and lose capacity over time. Chlorine and chloramine in city water also slowly break resin down. We run a resin cleaner through the system and test performance afterward. If the resin is exhausted, we rebed the tank with fresh high-capacity resin, which costs far less than a new system.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Charleston, WV

    The brine tank is crucial for salt dissolution and regeneration. Over time, salt residue and other impurities can accumulate, forming a 'salt bridge' that prevents the system from properly regenerating. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening. This is a common issue we address in Charleston homes.

    Valve and Controller Diagnostics in Charleston, WV

    The control valve and electronic controller regulate the entire softening process. Our technicians perform diagnostic checks to confirm these components are operating accurately, adjusting programming as needed. We check for wear and tear on moving parts and lubricate them to prevent future issues, keeping your water softener dependable in Charleston, WV.

    Resin Bed Analysis and Regeneration in Charleston

    The resin bed is the heart of your water softener, where ion exchange occurs. During maintenance, we check the condition of the resin beads and confirm the regeneration cycles are programmed correctly for your household's water usage. Proper resin bed function is key to steady soft water throughout your home in Charleston.

    Control Valve and Injector Service in Charleston, WV

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a full regeneration every time.

    Regeneration Settings and Hardness Testing in Charleston, WV

    Many softeners are set incorrectly from day one or never updated when household size changes. We test your raw water hardness and program the valve to match your actual use. Metered systems regenerate based on gallons used, which saves salt compared to time clock models. Correct settings keep water soft without wasting salt or water.
