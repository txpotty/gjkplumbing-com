    Leaking Disposal Repair in Charleston, WV

    Leaks can come from the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube or the disposal body itself. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty, and replace gaskets or tubes when needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed. In that case, replacement is the smarter option.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Charleston

    A humming disposal that will not spin usually has a jam. Bones, fruit pits, glass or utensils can wedge the flywheel. We cut power, use a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit to free the flywheel and remove the object. Then we test the motor and press the reset button if needed.
