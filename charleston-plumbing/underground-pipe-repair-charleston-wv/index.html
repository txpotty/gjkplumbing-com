    Whether an underground pipe leak is covered by insurance depends on your homeowner's policy and the cause of the damage. Typically, sudden and accidental damage may be covered, but issues from normal wear and tear or neglect are usually not.     Ignoring an underground pipe leak can lead to significant problems, including costly water damage to your foundation, landscaping erosion, mold growth, higher water bills and potential contamination of your water supply. It's crucial to address these issues promptly.

    Common signs include a water bill that jumps without explanation, a meter that moves with all water off, low pressure and wet or sinking spots in the yard. A hissing sound near the meter box can also point to a leak.

    Common signs of a broken underground pipe include unexplained wet spots or lush patches in your yard, a sudden drop in water pressure, unusual odors such as sulfur or sewage, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets or an unexpected increase in your water bill. If you notice any of these, it's smart to call for a professional inspection promptly.
