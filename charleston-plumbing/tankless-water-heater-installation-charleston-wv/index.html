    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Charleston, WV

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Many West Virginia homes rely on private wells or springs, and that water can carry iron, manganese or sulfur that discolors fixtures and laundry. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.
