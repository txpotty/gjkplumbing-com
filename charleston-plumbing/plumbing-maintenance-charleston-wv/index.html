    Backflow Preventer Testing in Charleston

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers require annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where necessary. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Charleston

    Regular water heater maintenance is crucial to preventing sediment buildup. Testing well water every year or so is a good habit in West Virginia, especially after flooding or nearby ground disturbance. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, improves efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.

    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Charleston

    Aging galvanized pipe clogs with rust and scale over time, causing low water pressure, leaks and discolored water. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are typical signs. Finding a leak early prevents structural damage and mold.

    Repiping and Polybutylene Replacement in Charleston, WV

    Homes with polybutylene or aging galvanized pipe face a higher risk of leaks and low water pressure. During maintenance, we check pipe materials and look for signs of failure. If repiping makes sense, we explain PEX and copper options and give a detailed estimate. Repiping can be done in stages to fit your budget. New pipe brings back strong pressure and peace of mind.

    Sewer Line and Drain Camera Inspection in Charleston, WV

    A camera inspection shows the condition of your sewer line and main drains. We look for roots, bellies, cracks and buildup before they cause a blockage. Older homes with clay or cast iron lines benefit most from regular checks. We record the inspection and give you a copy. If cleaning or repair is needed, we explain your options.

    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Charleston, WV

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits greatly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to early tank failure.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Charleston, WV

    Flooded basements and sewer backups are common after major storms, so backwater valves and sump pumps are worthwhile upgrades.     Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Charleston
