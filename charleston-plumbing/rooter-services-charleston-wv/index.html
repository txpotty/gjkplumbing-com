    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Charleston

    The cost of rooter services in Charleston, WV can vary a lot, typically ranging from $150 to $500 for standard drain snaking, depending on the severity and location of the clog. We provide upfront pricing after a thorough diagnosis, so you know the cost before any work begins.

    While chemical drain cleaners are available, GJK Plumbing generally advises against them. They often only partially clear clogs, can damage pipes over time, and carry safety risks. Professional rooter services are much more effective, safer for your plumbing, and provide a lasting solution to blockages.

    Snaking uses an auger to break through and pull out blockages, while hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the entire inside of the pipe, removing buildup like grease and mineral deposits. Hydro-jetting provides a far more thorough cleaning, often recommended for recurring clogs or main sewer lines.

    We use industry-leading equipment for our rooter services. Our technicians commonly use professional-grade tools from brands like RIDGID, Spartan Tool, and Gorlitz for drain augers, sewer cameras, and hydro-jetting machines. These brands are known for their reliability and performance in tough plumbing situations.

    Rooting uses a spinning cable with cutting blades to break up clogs and roots. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the pipe walls. Many tough lines benefit from both.

    Common signs include slow drains, gurgling sounds from pipes, water backing up in sinks or toilets, and foul odors coming from drains. If you notice any of these issues, especially multiple fixtures backing up, it's a strong sign you need professional rooter service.

    To prevent future clogs, avoid pouring grease down drains, use drain screens in sinks and showers to catch hair and food particles, and dispose of fibrous materials like coffee grounds and wipes in the trash. Consider periodic professional drain maintenance or hydro-jetting, especially for older homes or those with a history of recurring blockages.
