    Galvanized steel pipe was common in homes built before the 1970s. Over time, it corrodes inside and narrows the pipe. This causes low pressure and rusty water. We replace galvanized lines with modern PEX or copper. The difference in water pressure is often noticeable right away.

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a modern, flexible, and increasingly popular choice for water line replacement. PEX can expand and contract without bursting, which is a real advantage when a cold snap hits Charleston. Its flexibility allows for fewer fittings and quicker installation.
