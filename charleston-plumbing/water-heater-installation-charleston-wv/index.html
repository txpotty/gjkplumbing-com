    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Charleston, WV

    Electric tank water heaters are common in homes without natural gas service. We install models with the correct voltage and breaker size for your electrical panel. These units do not need venting, which allows more flexible placement. We also install a drain pan when the heater is in a closet or attic. Electric models are simple, reliable and quiet.

    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Charleston

    Businesses in Charleston have unique hot water needs, often requiring larger, more heavy-duty commercial-grade water heaters.     Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Charleston, WV

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from surrounding air, which can make them efficient in warm garages and utility rooms. We install models from Rheem and A.O. Smith with proper condensate drainage. These units need adequate air space and clearance. They may qualify for utility rebates or tax credits. We help you decide if a hybrid is right for your home.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Charleston

    Many homes in Charleston use natural gas for their water heaters due to its efficiency and low operating costs. When replacing a gas water heater, precise gas line connections, proper ventilation and carbon monoxide detection are essential. Our licensed plumbers are experts in gas appliance installation, ensuring every safety measure is met. We check for leaks, test the igniter and confirm proper exhaust, following all local West Virginia codes. You can trust us to safely and effectively install your new gas water heater, providing reliable hot water.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Charleston, WV

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and plumbing.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Charleston, WV

    Tankless water heaters provide on-demand hot water, saving energy and space, and they are more and more popular in West Virginia homes. Our experts specialize in installing these highly efficient units, making sure of proper gas line sizing or electrical service upgrades if needed. We also address venting requirements, which are often different from traditional units. While the upfront cost can be higher, the long-term energy savings and endless hot water supply make them an attractive option for many homeowners in Charleston. We'll help you choose the right model for your specific needs.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Charleston

    The cost of water heater installation in Charleston, WV can range from $1,200 to $4,000 or more, depending on the type of unit (traditional tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any necessary plumbing or electrical upgrades. Tankless units generally have higher installation costs due to more complex venting and utility requirements. We provide a written, upfront quote after assessing your specific needs.

    Many tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years, though hard water can shorten that. Regular flushing and anode rod checks help extend life.

    While it might seem like a DIY project, water heater installation involves working with gas lines, high voltage electricity and water connections, all of which carry significant risks if not handled correctly. Improper installation can lead to leaks, gas explosions, electrical fires and carbon monoxide poisoning. It can also void your warranty, and local codes typically require a licensed plumber for this work.

    We install Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White, Navien, Rinnai and other trusted brands. We recommend models based on reliability, warranty and budget.

    To improve efficiency, make sure your new water heater is properly sized for your home, consider a tankless model for on-demand heating, insulate hot water pipes and set the thermostat to 120°F (49°C) to prevent scalding and cut energy consumption.

    Yes, we stand behind our work. Our water heater installations come with a labor warranty, and the water heater unit itself will have a manufacturer's warranty, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years, depending on the model.

    In most areas, a plumbing permit is required for water heater replacement or installation to confirm the work meets local building and safety codes. Water heater replacements, sewer repairs and new plumbing in additions generally require a permit where local code enforcement applies. GJK Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications and inspections in Charleston as part of our service.

    The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand. For tank units, a 40 to 50 gallon tank is common for 2 to 4 people, while larger families might need 60 to 80 gallons. For tankless units, it is about flow rate (GPM) and temperature rise.
