    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Beckley, WV

    Sewer lines in Beckley, WV can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate. Basements and crawlspaces are both common in West Virginia homes, and hillside lots often need thoughtful planning for sewer and drain routing. Tree root intrusion is another major cause, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also add to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Sectional Point Repair in Beckley

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are a good fit on otherwise healthy lines.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Beckley

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot get into. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their form.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Cost in Beckley, WV

    Point repairs often cost $1,500 to $3,500, while full lining of a residential lateral often runs $4,000 to $12,000 depending on length and diameter. Pipe bursting typically ranges from $5,000 to $15,000. Traditional excavation can be cheaper upfront on short, shallow runs, but restoring concrete and landscaping can quickly close the gap. We price both options so you can decide.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Beckley

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for extensive trenching. It is best for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Beckley that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Beckley, WV

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is well suited for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a quick and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by West Virginia homeowners.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Beckley

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while fracturing the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is highly resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Beckley homes. This method is well suited when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.
