    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Beckley

    Softener resin can foul with iron, manganese or sediment and lose capacity over time. Chlorine and chloramine in city water also slowly break resin down. We run a resin cleaner through the system and test performance afterward. If the resin is exhausted, we rebed the tank with fresh high-capacity resin, which costs far less than a new system.

    Routine Water Softener Tune-Ups in Beckley

    Our routine tune-ups involve a thorough inspection of your entire water softener system. We check for proper salt levels, inspect the brine tank for salt bridges and confirm the resin bed is working effectively. This preventive service is vital wherever hard water minerals can quickly affect system performance if not regularly addressed.

    Valve and Controller Diagnostics in Beckley, WV

    The control valve and electronic controller regulate the entire softening process. Our technicians perform diagnostic checks to confirm these components are operating accurately, adjusting programming as needed. We check for wear and tear on moving parts and lubricate them to prevent future issues, keeping your water softener dependable in Beckley, WV.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Beckley

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, controlling regeneration cycles and water flow. Our maintenance service includes complete diagnostics of the control valve's programming and mechanical components. We check for proper timing, motor function and potential blockages. If issues are found, we can often repair or replace worn parts to restore full function, preventing the need for a costly full system replacement in Beckley.

    Regeneration Settings and Hardness Testing in Beckley, WV

    Many softeners are set incorrectly from day one or never updated when household size changes. We test your raw water hardness and program the valve to match your actual use. Metered systems regenerate based on gallons used, which saves salt compared to time clock models. Correct settings keep water soft without wasting salt or water.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Maintenance in Beckley

    Template assisted crystallization and other salt-free conditioners do not remove hardness but change how it behaves. They still need media replacement every few years and sediment prefilter changes much more often. We inspect the media tank, replace prefilters and check bypass valves. We also explain honestly how these systems differ from a true softener.

    Control Valve and Injector Service in Beckley, WV

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw.
