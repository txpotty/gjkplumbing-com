    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Charles Town

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for truly softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. GJK Plumbing expertly installs many models of salt-based softeners, ensuring proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Charles Town home.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Charles Town

    Accurate testing is essential for proper softener settings. We test your water for hardness, iron and chlorine before installation. Then we program regeneration frequency and salt dosage. This helps reduce salt use while keeping water soft. We review settings with you so you understand how the system works.

    Water Softener Replacement in Charles Town, WV

    Older softeners can lose efficiency due to worn resin, failing valves or clogged injectors. We remove the old unit and install a new, more efficient model.     Softener Loop Installation in Charles Town

    A softener loop is the plumbing connection that routes your main water line through a softener. Many newer homes include a loop, but older homes often do not.
