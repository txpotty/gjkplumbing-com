    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Charles Town

    Tankless units are especially sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures consistent.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Charles Town

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Charles Town, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's significantly depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing expensive tank leaks.

Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.
