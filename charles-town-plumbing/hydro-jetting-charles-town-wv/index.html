    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Charles Town

    Residential hydro jetting in Charles Town typically costs $350 to $800, depending on line length, access and how heavy the buildup is. Commercial jetting is often priced by the hour and may run higher.

    A standard residential hydro jetting service in Charles Town usually takes between 1 and 3 hours. This includes the initial camera inspection, the jetting process itself, and a final inspection. More extensive commercial jobs or severe main line blockages might need more time.

    Small consumer jetters exist, but they lack the pressure and flow of professional units. High-pressure water can also injure untrained users and damage fragile pipe.

    Most residential jobs take 1 to 3 hours including inspection. Long commercial lines or heavy grease can add time.

    Hydro jetting is exceptionally effective against almost all types of clogs. It can break through solid obstructions like tree roots, flush out heavy grease and food buildup, clear mineral scale, and remove compacted sludge and debris that traditional snaking often leaves behind.

    No permit is needed for jetting. If the inspection reveals damage that needs repair, permits may be required and we handle them.

    Snaking uses a rotating cable to break through a clog. Jetting uses pressurized water to clean the entire pipe wall and wash debris away.

    For most modern and well-maintained pipes, hydro jetting is very safe. However, for very old, corroded, or severely damaged pipes, the high pressure could cause further damage. That is why GJK Plumbing always performs a camera inspection first to assess your pipe's condition and confirm hydro jetting is appropriate.
