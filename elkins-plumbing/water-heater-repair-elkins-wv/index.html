    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Repair in Elkins

    Most water heater repairs run between $150 and $600. Thermocouple and element replacements sit at the lower end, while gas control valves and tankless sensors cost more. We quote the full price before starting.

    Testing well water every year or so is a good habit in West Virginia, especially after flooding or nearby ground disturbance. With hard water, once a year is a good rule for tank heaters. Tankless units should be descaled about once a year as well, or more often if you have no water softener.

    Popping or rumbling usually comes from sediment on the tank bottom. Water trapped beneath the scale boils and pops. A professional flush usually quiets the tank and improves efficiency.

    Regular maintenance, including annual flushing of the tank to remove sediment, checking the anode rod, and inspecting the pressure relief valve, can greatly extend your water heater's lifespan and prevent many common issues. Consider a maintenance plan with GJK Plumbing.

    That smell typically comes from a reaction between bacteria in the water and the magnesium anode rod. Swapping to an aluminum zinc or powered anode and flushing the tank often solves it.
