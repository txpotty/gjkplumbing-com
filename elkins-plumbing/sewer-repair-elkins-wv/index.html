    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Elkins

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to recurring clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it will hold.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Elkins, WV

    Tree roots are a common cause of sewer line blockages and damage, especially with the mature trees found in many Elkins neighborhoods. Roots can work into tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog pipes. We use specialized augers and hydro-jetting to cut through and remove roots, followed by inspection to assess pipe integrity. For ongoing issues, we may recommend trenchless repair or pipe replacement to prevent future root problems.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Elkins, WV

    While often overlooked, the sewer vent pipe is essential for proper drainage and preventing sewer gas buildup. Issues with vent pipes, such as blockages from animal nests or damage from storms, can lead to slow drains, gurgling noises and foul odors. GJK Plumbing diagnoses and repairs vent pipe problems, making sure your entire drainage system works correctly and safely and protecting good indoor air quality.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Elkins, WV

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Elkins

    When trenchless repair isn't possible due to severe damage or specific pipe configurations, traditional dig-and-replace methods are necessary. This involves excavating a trench to access and replace the damaged section of the sewer line. While more intrusive, it is sometimes the only way to address severely collapsed pipes, significant offsets or certain pipe materials common in older West Virginia properties. Our team ensures careful excavation and efficient replacement, minimizing the impact on your property in Elkins.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Elkins

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can usually use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Elkins, WV

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Elkins.
