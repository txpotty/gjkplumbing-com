    Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in Elkins

    Cured-in-place pipe lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. After cleaning, a resin-soaked liner is pulled in and cured in place. This works when the host pipe still holds its shape without major sags or collapse. Lining can be a good option under slabs or finished areas. We tell you plainly if your line is a candidate.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Elkins, WV

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we close it up.
