    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Huntington

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Huntington. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. GJK Plumbing focuses on precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with trusted brands like Watts and Febco.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Huntington

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.
