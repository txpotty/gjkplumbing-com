    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Huntington, WV

    Calcium scale is the leading cause of tankless heater failure. Water hardness in West Virginia ranges from moderate to hard in many areas, and mineral scale is a common issue for water heaters and fixtures. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides extra protection.
