    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Huntington

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation. We route rigid PVC discharge pipe out of the basement and extend it at least 10 feet from the house or into a pop-up emitter or dry well. A spring or swing check valve keeps the column of water from dropping back into the pit after every cycle, which saves wear on the motor. Higher elevations see harsher and longer freezes, so pipe insulation, heat tape and frost-free spigots are common precautions. Where lines can freeze, we add a freeze-resistant outlet so ice cannot block the discharge.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Huntington

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a good fit for narrow pits in older homes. They usually last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we generally recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod set to your pit depth.
