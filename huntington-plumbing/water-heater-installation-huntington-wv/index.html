    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Huntington

    The cost of water heater installation in Huntington, WV can range from $1,200 to $4,000 or more, depending on the type of unit (traditional tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed plumbing or electrical upgrades. Tankless units generally have higher installation costs due to more complex venting and utility requirements. We provide a written, upfront quote after assessing your specific needs.

    An expansion tank absorbs excess water pressure created as water heats up inside the tank. Many homes in Huntington, WV, particularly those with closed-loop plumbing systems, are required by code to have an expansion tank to protect the water heater and plumbing system from damage.

    Common signs that you might need a new water heater include a lack of hot water, rusty or discolored water coming from the hot tap, banging or rumbling noises from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit and an age of 10 to 15 years or more. If you notice any of these issues, it is wise to have a professional inspection.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for water heater replacement. We pull permits when required and confirm the install meets code.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 6 to 12 years on the tank and parts. We also warranty our installation labor.

    Most standard water heater installations, especially for replacing a similar tank unit, can be completed within 3 to 4 hours. However, a conversion from a tank to a tankless system, or installations requiring major plumbing or electrical modifications, might take a full day or longer. We will provide an estimated timeline during our first assessment.

    New water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. GJK Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, giving you peace of mind. We will discuss specific warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

    To improve efficiency, make sure your new water heater is properly sized for your home, consider a tankless model for on-demand heating, insulate hot water pipes and set the thermostat to 120°F (49°C) to prevent scalding and reduce energy consumption.
