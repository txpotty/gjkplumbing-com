    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in Huntington, WV

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement swaps out pipes without extensive digging. We offer pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward. This is a great fit when protecting landscaping or driveways is a priority. The method works well for many homes in Huntington and can often be completed faster than traditional methods, using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe known for its long life.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Huntington, WV

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. We dig along the line, remove the old pipe and set new PVC on compacted bedding at the correct grade. Rocky or hard-packed soil can slow digging, and we account for that in the estimate. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we cover it.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Line Replacement in Huntington

    For major damage or certain pipe layouts, traditional trenching may be the most effective method. This involves digging a trench to access and replace the damaged sewer line directly. Our team carefully plans the excavation to minimize disruption to your landscape, using the right equipment for efficient, precise digging. We make sure of proper grading and backfilling to prevent future issues and restore your property.

    Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in Huntington

    Cured-in-place pipe lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. After cleaning, a resin-soaked liner is pulled in and cured in place. This works when the host pipe still holds its shape without major sags or collapse. Lining can be a good option under slabs or finished areas. We tell you plainly if your line is a candidate.

    Sewer Line Replacement Cost in Huntington, WV

    Cost depends on length, depth, soil type, access and the method used. Open-trench replacement in an open yard can be quite affordable per foot, while trenchless methods may cost more per foot but save on restoration. Hardscape, landscaping and driveways change the math fast. We give you a written estimate for each option after a camera inspection. That way you can compare total cost, not just the headline price.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Huntington

    Pipe bursting replaces the old sewer line by pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one. A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while fused HDPE pipe follows behind. This typically needs only an entry pit and an exit pit instead of a long trench. It works well under driveways, mature trees and landscaping. The new pipe is seamless, which leaves no joints for roots to enter.
