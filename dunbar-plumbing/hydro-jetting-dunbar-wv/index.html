    Sewer Line Descaling with Hydro Jetting in Dunbar

    Over time, mineral deposits and other materials can build up on the interior walls of sewer lines, forming a tough scale. This reduces pipe diameter and can lead to repeat clogs. Many West Virginia homes rely on private wells or springs, and that water can carry iron, manganese or sulfur that discolors fixtures and laundry. Hydro jetting is the most effective method for sewer line descaling, using powerful water jets to break down and flush away these mineral deposits, restoring the pipe's full carrying capacity and slowing future buildup.

    Residential Sewer Line Jetting in Dunbar

    Main sewer lines in homes are usually 3 or 4 inches and can collect years of grease, soap, mineral scale and roots. We feed jetting hose through a cleanout and work it toward the street, cleaning in passes. Rear-facing jets pull the nozzle forward while forward jets break up heavy blockages.     Scale Removal in Cast Iron Pipes in Dunbar

    Cast iron drains in older homes build up rust tubercles and mineral scale, and hard water speeds that process. Jetting with the right nozzle can strip much of this buildup and restore flow. We keep pressure in a safe range for aged iron and watch the camera for thin spots. This cleaning is also the standard prep step before pipe lining. Clean pipe walls help a liner bond properly.

    Hydro Jetting Before Pipe Lining in Dunbar, WV

    Cured-in-place pipe lining needs a clean surface to adhere and seal correctly. We jet the host pipe to remove roots, scale and debris, then camera it to confirm it is ready. Any heavy obstructions are cut out before the liner is installed. Skipping this step can lead to wrinkles or poor bonding. Proper prep is part of why lined pipe can last many years.
