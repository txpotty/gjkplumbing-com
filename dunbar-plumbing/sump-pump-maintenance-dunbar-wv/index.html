    Flooded basements and sewer backups are common after major storms, so backwater valves and sump pumps are worthwhile upgrades.     Battery Backup System Testing in Dunbar, WV

    A power outage during a heavy storm can render your primary sump pump useless. Our maintenance service includes complete testing of your battery backup sump pump system.     Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Dunbar, WV

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil near the slab or foundation.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Dunbar, WV

    The float switch is a critical component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to ensure reliable operation for your home in Dunbar, WV.
