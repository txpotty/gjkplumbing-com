    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can build one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, compacted clay or rock and set a durable basin.     Sump Pump Replacement in Dunbar, WV

    Sump pumps often sit idle for long stretches between storms, which can lead to stuck floats and seized motors.
