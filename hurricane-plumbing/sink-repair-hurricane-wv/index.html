    Supply Line and Shutoff Valve Repair in Hurricane, WV

    Braided supply lines and angle stops are rated for years, not decades, and the rubber inside breaks down in warm cabinets. A failing line can burst without warning and flood a bathroom.     Overflow and Drain Body Leak Repair in Hurricane, WV

    Water that appears only when the sink is full often comes from the overflow gasket or the drain flange putty. We pull the drain, clean the old putty or sealant, and reset the flange with fresh plumber's putty or silicone based on the basin material. Then we fill the bowl past the overflow and check for drips. This kind of leak is easy to miss and easy to fix once found.

    Slow Drain and Clog Clearing in Hurricane

    Bathroom sinks clog with hair, soap scum and toothpaste mixed with mineral scale.     Loose or Cracked Basin Repair in Hurricane

    Undermount sinks are held by clips and epoxy that can let go, leaving a gap and a leak along the counter.     Leaking P-Trap Repair in Hurricane
