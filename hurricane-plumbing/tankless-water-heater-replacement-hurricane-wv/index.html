    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Hurricane

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by recovering heat from exhaust gases. They vent through low-cost PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Frequent freeze and thaw cycles in the mountains stress fittings and crack exposed pipes over time. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Hurricane, WV

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. Exterior models are often the simplest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.
