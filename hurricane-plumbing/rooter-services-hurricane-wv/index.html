    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Hurricane

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet typically costs $125 to $300. Main sewer line rooting through a cleanout usually runs $200 to $500. Adding hydro jetting brings the total to about $450 to $1,000.

    Most standard rooter services for isolated clogs can be completed in 1 to 3 hours. More complex main sewer line blockages, especially those involving extensive root removal or hydro-jetting, might take longer, sometimes up to a full day. We aim for efficient service with lasting results.

    We use top professional-grade equipment, including Spartan Tool and Ridgid rooter machines and hydro-jetters. These brands are known for their power and reliability, allowing us to tackle the toughest blockages without damaging your plumbing system.

    To prevent future clogs, avoid pouring grease down drains, use drain screens in sinks and showers to catch hair and food particles, and throw away fibrous materials like coffee grounds and wipes in the trash. Consider periodic professional drain maintenance or hydro-jetting, especially for older homes or those with a history of recurring blockages.

    Rental machines are available, but they can kink, grab and injure users who are not trained. They can also get stuck or damage older pipe. Professional rooter service is safer and usually more effective.

    Drain snaking, also known as augering, uses a long, flexible cable with a cutting head to physically break through or retrieve blockages like hair or tree roots. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to clean the entire interior of the pipe, removing grease, mineral buildup, and sludge, leaving pipes much cleaner than snaking alone. Hydro-jetting is generally more effective for severe or recurring clogs.

    Most standard rooter services for localized clogs can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. More complex issues, such as main sewer line root removal or extensive hydro-jetting, may take longer, potentially 3 to 6 hours. We aim to complete the job efficiently while staying thorough.

    While chemical drain cleaners are available, GJK Plumbing generally advises against them. They often only partially clear clogs, can damage pipes over time, and carry safety risks. Professional rooter services are much more effective, safer for your plumbing, and provide a lasting solution to blockages.
