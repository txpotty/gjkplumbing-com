    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Hurricane, WV

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Hurricane

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Many West Virginia homes rely on private wells or springs, and that water can carry iron, manganese or sulfur that stains fixtures and laundry. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more often follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and reduces the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Hurricane

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a good option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer reliable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to verify a proper seal.
