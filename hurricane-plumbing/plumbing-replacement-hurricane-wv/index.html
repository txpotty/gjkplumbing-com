    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Replacement in Hurricane

    Costs vary by job. A tank water heater replacement usually runs $1,400 to $3,000, a water softener $1,500 to $3,500, a water line $1,800 to $5,500 and a sewer line $4,000 to $15,000 or more.

    Many sewer line replacements take one to three days depending on length, depth and method. Trenchless options can shorten the timeline.

    Most tank water heater replacements take two to four hours. Code upgrades or relocations can take longer.

    Climate, freezes and soil type all affect how long plumbing lasts. Frequent freeze and thaw cycles in the mountains loosen fittings and crack exposed pipes over time. Freeze-thaw cycles can damage pipes, and corrosive or shifting soils can speed up pipe degradation. Shale and clay soils can hold water and move over time, which can crack sewer lines and push water into basements. Our replacement solutions are chosen to withstand these local conditions for lasting durability.

    Key signs include frequent, recurring leaks in the same area, discolored water, very low water pressure throughout the house, extensive corrosion visible on pipes or a major component like a water heater being over 10 to 15 years old and failing. If repair costs approach the cost of replacement, replacement is often the smarter long-term investment.

    We mainly use PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) and copper piping for water supply lines. For sewer and drain lines, we use durable PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene) pipes, based on the application and local codes.
