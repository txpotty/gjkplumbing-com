    Water Pressure Restoration in Hurricane

    Low water pressure can be a frustrating symptom of an underlying water line problem, such as blockages, leaks, or a failing pressure regulator. In Hurricane, if your water pressure has dropped suddenly, it's crucial to have your main water line inspected.     Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Hurricane

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a especially challenging issue. Aging galvanized pipe narrows with rust and scale over time, causing low water pressure, leaks and discolored water. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to serious structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to locate slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak promptly in Hurricane is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Hurricane, WV

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Many West Virginia homes rely on private wells or springs, and that water can carry iron, manganese or sulfur that stains fixtures and laundry. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which restricts flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Hurricane

    Whether your home has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our technicians are skilled in repairing both.
