    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Hurricane

    A standard single tank softener replacement usually costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can raise the cost.

    GJK Plumbing installs a wide range of trusted water softener brands, including Fleck, Autotrol, Culligan, Pelican and Watts. We select brands known for their reliability, efficiency and long-term performance, so you get a quality system that meets your needs.

    Salt-based softeners remove hardness minerals, calcium and magnesium, through ion exchange, truly softening the water. Salt-free conditioners do not remove minerals but change their crystal structure so they do not stick to surfaces. That prevents scale buildup without actually softening the water.

    Spots on dishes, scale on faucets, dry skin and increased soap use can signal a failing softener. A softener that no longer uses salt or keeps flooding the brine tank may also need replacement.

    With proper maintenance, most quality water softeners can last between 10 and 15 years, and sometimes even longer. Factors like water hardness, usage volume and the quality of the original installation can affect its lifespan. Regular servicing helps extend the life of your unit.
