    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Hurricane

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most effective method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Shale and clay soils can hold water and shift over time, which can crack sewer lines and push water into basements. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also integrated to divert water away.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Hurricane

    Many homes in Hurricane have crawl spaces, which are very prone to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting prevents ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an affordable way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Hurricane, WV

    Washing machine hoses, utility sinks and water heaters can all leak into nearby walls. We replace burst-prone rubber hoses with braided stainless hoses and install leak pans and shutoff valves. Walls around water heaters and laundry boxes can be sealed to resist water damage. These small upgrades prevent costly drywall repairs.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Hurricane, WV

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already penetrated the foundation walls. This usually involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it effectively stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and works well where exterior access is limited.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Hurricane, WV

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are frequent culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry for good.
