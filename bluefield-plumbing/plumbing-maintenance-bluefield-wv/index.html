    Water Softener Maintenance in Bluefield

    Water softeners fight hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale.     Backflow Preventer Testing in Bluefield

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers require annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Bluefield

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility right away.
