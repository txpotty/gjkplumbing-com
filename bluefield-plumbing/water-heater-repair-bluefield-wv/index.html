    Most repairs are finished in one visit lasting one to two hours. If a special part must be ordered, we typically have it within one to three business days.

    Regular maintenance, including annual flushing of the tank to remove sediment, checking the anode rod, and inspecting the pressure relief valve, can significantly extend your water heater's lifespan and prevent many common issues. Ask about a maintenance plan with GJK Plumbing.

    That smell typically comes from a reaction between bacteria in the water and the magnesium anode rod. Swapping to an aluminum zinc or powered anode and flushing the tank often solves it.

    Popping or rumbling typically comes from sediment on the tank bottom. Water trapped beneath the scale boils and pops. A professional flush usually quiets the tank and improves efficiency.

    While some minor issues like resetting a tripped breaker or relighting a pilot light might be attempted by a homeowner, most water heater repairs are complex and better handled by licensed professionals.
