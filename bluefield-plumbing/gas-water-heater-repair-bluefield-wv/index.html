    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Bluefield

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it senses vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and shut down the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as required.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Bluefield, WV

    Atmospheric gas water heaters rely on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and confirm draft with a test. Proper venting is critical for your family's safety.

    Gas Water Heater Code Compliance in Bluefield, WV

    Any repair involving gas appliances must follow strict local and state plumbing codes. GJK Plumbing ensures all gas water heater repairs in Bluefield, WV are performed in full compliance with current regulations. This includes proper venting, gas line connections and seismic strapping where applicable. Our adherence to code ensures not only safety but also a smooth path through any future inspections.

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Bluefield

    Rural properties often run water heaters on propane instead of natural gas. Propane units use different orifices and regulator pressures, and improper conversions cause sooting and poor performance. We confirm the correct parts are installed and repair pilots, valves and burners. We also check tank regulators for proper pressure.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Bluefield, WV

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame signals a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed. A clean blue flame heats faster and produces less carbon monoxide. We also check for rust flakes falling from the flue.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Bluefield, WV

    The gas control valve regulates temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something fails. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or listed universal model. We leak test and verify temperature control afterward.

    Pilot Light and Burner Issues in Bluefield

    A frequent problem with gas water heaters is a pilot light that won't stay lit or a burner that fails to ignite. This can be caused by a faulty thermocouple, a dirty pilot tube or issues with the gas control valve. Our technicians in Bluefield are skilled at diagnosing these components and performing the necessary repairs, whether it is cleaning, adjusting or replacing parts. We confirm proper gas flow and ignition for consistent hot water.
